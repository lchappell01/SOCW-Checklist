Communication Skills Checklist

This checklist helps you identify the specific communication skills you may want to develop and use in your relationships. You will rate yourself on 23 skills across five areas: Listening and Understanding, Expressing Positives, Talking About Feelings, Expressing Negative Feelings, and Making Requests.

Instructions
  • Each statement will appear one at a time. Rate how well you use that skill on a scale of 1 (Not So Well) to 5 (Very Well).
  • Answer honestly, based on how you communicate now.
  • The checklist does not produce a score. When you finish, your skills will be organized to show where you may want to focus your growth.
Keyboard Navigation
  • Press Tab to move between the rating options and buttons.
  • When focused on the rating options, use the Arrow keys to move between choices and select your rating.
  • Press Enter or Space to activate buttons.

Feldman, C. M. The Therapist’s Toolkit. Adapted and expanded from P. H. Neidig & D. H. Friedman (1984).
